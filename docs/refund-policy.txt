AutoMind Inc. — Refund Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. General Refund Policy
All customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are prorated based on the remaining subscription period. No refunds are issued after 90 days.

2. How to Request a Refund
To request a refund, contact support@automind.ai with your order number and reason for the refund. Refund requests are processed within 5-7 business days.

3. Non-Refundable Items
The following are non-refundable:
- Setup fees for Enterprise plans
- Custom integration development costs
- Training session fees once the session has been delivered
- API usage charges that have already been consumed

4. Subscription Cancellation
You may cancel your subscription at any time from the Account Settings page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You will retain access to all features until the billing period ends.

5. Enterprise Refund Policy
Enterprise customers with annual contracts may request a refund within the first 60 days of the contract. After 60 days, early termination fees of 25% of the remaining contract value apply. Contact your account manager for details.

6. Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Credit card refunds appear within 5-10 business days. Wire transfer refunds may take up to 15 business days.
